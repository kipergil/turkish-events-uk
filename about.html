About this site

Turkish Events UK brings Turkish, Turkish Cypriot and Türkiye-related events together in one place, starting with London, so you don't have to follow dozens of pages, groups and ticket sites to know what's on.

Every morning it collects new listings from public event pages, removes events that have finished and refreshes the feed. It's free to use and you don't need an account.

The boxes at the top give a quick count from the feed: upcoming events, events this weekend, venues and London areas. Tap "upcoming events" or "this weekend" to jump to that list.

What you'll find

  • Concerts and live music
  • Parties, club nights and meyhane nights
  • Talks, lectures and book events
  • Workshops, classes and courses
  • Exhibitions and museum tours
  • Film screenings
  • Theatre and stand-up comedy
  • Football matches, match screenings and other sport
  • Festivals, fairs and carnivals
  • Faith and spiritual gatherings
  • Embassy and official events open to the public
  • Business and networking events
  • Community and family events

Each type has its own colour, and you'll see that colour on the filters, on each event, in the calendar and on the map.

How to use it

Pick a time
Tap a date button, or open the date menu if you use menus, and choose Today, Tomorrow, This weekend (Friday to Sunday), This week, Next week or This month. "Any time" shows everything coming up. Each choice shows how many events it has.
Pick a type
Tap a type button, or open the type menu, and choose a type, such as Music or Sport, to see only those events. The number next to each type shows how many events match. Once chosen, the dot and the menu take that type's colour, and each event shows its type, in its colour, under the start time.
Clear a filter
Tap the selected button again, or the ✕ on it or inside a menu, to remove that filter. Choosing "Any time" or "All types" works too.
Pills or menus
Dates and types show as rows of buttons. Prefer something more compact? Tap Menus next to the view switcher to turn them into two dropdown menus, with the same counts and colours. Tap Pills to switch back. Your choice is remembered on this device.
Search
Type an artist, venue, area or keyword, for example "meyhane", "Hackney" or "Galatasaray". Tap the ✕ in the search box to clear it.
Switch views
List shows events day by day, with exhibitions and courses that are already open listed after them. Calendar shows a month at a glance; tap a day to see its events. Map shows where events are; tap a pin to see what's on at that venue.
Open an event
Tap the event title or the View button (on the right of each event) to go to the organiser's page for full details and tickets.
Save it
Add to calendar saves the event in your Google Calendar. Venue on map opens the venue in Google Maps for directions. On small screens these buttons are shortened to "Calendar" and "Map".
Share it
The page address keeps your filters, so you can copy it and send a friend, for example "parties this weekend".

Before you go

Listings are gathered automatically and can change or contain errors. Always check the date, time, price and availability on the original event page, by tapping the event title or the View button, before you travel or buy tickets. This site doesn't sell tickets and isn't connected to the organisers.

Missing an event?

New events are picked up every morning. If something you know about isn't listed yet, check back tomorrow.
